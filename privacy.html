Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Tí Que Studio is a private tool that publishes our own videos to our own TikTok account.

What we access. When the account owner connects TikTok, we receive the account's basic profile (open ID, display name, username) and an access token, with the scopes user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload.

How we use it. Only to upload and publish videos to that same account and to check their publishing status.

Storage. Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. We do not collect data from other TikTok users or viewers.

Removal. The owner can disconnect at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) and by deleting the stored token.
